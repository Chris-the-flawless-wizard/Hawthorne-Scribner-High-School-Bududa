Holiday packages by class.

Each package is different. Nothing here is a repeat of the past-paper set.

S1 field sketch

Draw the path from home to the nearest market. Label three land uses. Write 80 words on who uses the path.

S2 kitchen science

Measure how fast a wet cloth dries in sun and shade. Record times in a table and state one fair-test rule.

S3 story map

Retell a family account of a harvest. Mark beginning, turn, and ending. No copied storybooks.

S4 mock bridge

One timed paper from the revision desk, then a one-page error log with the topic you will revisit.

S5 research card

Interview a grower about rainfall this year. Compare their notes with the school rain gauge chart.

S6 seminar brief

Prepare a five-minute talk on one UNEB topic you find slippery. Bring a single cue card, not a script.
