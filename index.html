Hawthorne-ScribnerHigh School · Bududa
Storeyed ridge campus · Est. 1986

Rise with the ridge.

A three-storey school above the coffee terraces of Bududa. Classes, houses, papers, film club, and music share one bright desk.

Join the school See the campus

The main block

Three floors, a clock gable, and steps that face Mount Elgon. Assembly meets on the lawn at 7:40.

Science floor

S2 to S6 practicals sit on the top floor. Groups are posted beside the physics lab.

Reading room

Two books at a time. Quiet hour is 7:00 to 8:30 on prep nights.

Pavilion

Netball lines were repainted this term. Falls House leads the table.

Day shape

Lessons start at 8:10. Break is 10:40. Lunch is 1:00. Games or clubs run until 4:30. Prep for boarders ends at 9:30.

Houses

Elgon wears violet, Coffee wears coral, Ridge wears mint, Falls wears gold. Points are read every Friday.

Morning ridge

Assembly faces Mount Elgon. Prefects read one notice, then classes begin at 8:10.

Paper room

Past papers are sorted by subject and class, with a worked sample inside each card.

Holiday desk

Each class has a distinct package: reading, a field sketch, and one family project.
